Rule ID: PROD_001

Product Volume shall be calculated as Product Length multiplied by Product Width multiplied by Product Height.

Rule ID: PROD_002

Product Weight must be greater than zero.

Rule ID: PROD_003

Product Density shall be calculated as Product Weight divided by Product Volume.

Rule ID: PROD_004

Product Dimensions (Length, Width, Height) must be greater than zero.

Rule ID: ORD_001

Delivery Duration shall be calculated as Customer Delivery Date minus Purchase Date.

Rule ID: ORD_002

Approval Duration shall be calculated as Approval Date minus Purchase Date.

Rule ID: ORD_003

Delivery Delay shall be calculated as Customer Delivery Date minus Estimated Delivery Date.

Rule ID: ORD_004

Delivery Status shall be classified as ON_TIME when Delivery Delay is less than or equal to zero, otherwise DELAYED.

Rule ID: PAY_001

Total Revenue shall be calculated using SUM(payment_value).

Rule ID: PAY_002

Average Installments shall be calculated using AVG(payment_installments).

Rule ID: PAY_003

Payment Count shall be calculated using COUNT(payment_sequential).

Rule ID: REV_001

Review Scores must be between 1 and 5.

Rule ID: REV_002

Review Category shall be classified as POSITIVE when Review Score is greater than or equal to 4.

Rule ID: REV_003

Review Category shall be classified as NEUTRAL when Review Score equals 3.

Rule ID: REV_004

Review Category shall be classified as NEGATIVE when Review Score is less than or equal to 2.

Rule ID: CUST_001

Customer State must not be null.

Rule ID: CUST_002

Customer Zip Code Prefix must be valid and non-null.

Rule ID: ITEM_001

Total Item Value shall be calculated using SUM(price).

Rule ID: ITEM_002

Average Freight Value shall be calculated using AVG(freight_value).

Rule ID: ITEM_003

Freight Value must be greater than zero.
